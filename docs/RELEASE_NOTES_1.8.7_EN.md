## SM64CDPY v1.8.7 — Two new themes and your own Retro Manga accent

Version 1.8.7 adds Minish Cap and Mario Kart 64 interface designs and lets you personalize the accent color of Retro Manga.

### Minish Cap

- Select Minish Cap in Settings for parchment-style surfaces, green and ochre details, and dedicated heading and body fonts.
- Light and dark modes share the same design language across navigation, settings and content.

### Mario Kart 64

- Select Mario Kart 64 in Settings for racing-style panels, checkered backgrounds and dedicated title typography.
- Both light and dark variants use lightweight native surfaces.

### Personalize Retro Manga

- Settings now offers cyan (original), blue, violet, pink, coral, amber, green and lime accents for Retro Manga.
- The selection is saved between sessions and retained when switching themes or brightness modes.
- Accent text adapts for readability; other themes, semantic colors and the floating overlay keep their own palettes.
